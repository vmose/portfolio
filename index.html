About

Who's behind the models

§ 01

I believe trustworthy data unlocks better decisions. Analytics engineering is where that trust gets built, modeling raw tables into datasets that are clean, tested, and documented enough for the rest of the business to rely on without double-checking.

My work lives in the warehouse: writing the transformation logic between raw sources and BI-ready tables, defining metrics once so they don't drift between dashboards, and making sure a broken model fails a test in CI, not a stakeholder's trust in the numbers.
